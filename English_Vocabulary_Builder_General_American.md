# English Vocabulary Builder — General American everyday edition

Source baseline: [English for Everyone: English Vocabulary Builder (Thomas Booth, DK)](https://newteacherlibraryandtools.square.site/uploads/b/e4443390-f724-11ec-9291-b5b724deb1dc/English%20for%20Everyone%20-%20English%20Vocabulary%20Builder%20%28Thomas%20Booth%2C%20DK%29%20%28z-lib.org%29%20%281%29.pdf)

This is a learner-facing General American update of the book vocabulary. The original extraction remains unchanged in English_Vocabulary_Builder_vocabularies.md so every source item can be audited. In this edition, the main list uses everyday U.S. wording and spelling where the book uses a British-first form; useful British forms are shown as recognition variants. Practical additions are marked separately.

## Editorial policy

- **American-facing vocabulary:** the normalized lesson list, including the book’s topic vocabulary.
- **Practical additions:** high-utility words and phrases for ordinary U.S. conversations, school, work, services, travel, health, and technology.
- **Recognition variants:** British or source forms that learners may still encounter; these are not “wrong.”
- **No silent deletion:** low-frequency, academic, scientific, and culturally specific source words remain in the lesson list. They are simply not treated as first-priority everyday additions.
- The complete rationale and source-to-American mapping are documented in English_Vocabulary_Builder_GA_change_log.md.
