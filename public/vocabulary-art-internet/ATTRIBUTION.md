# Daily Routines: Curated source illustrations

21 word-to-image matches from [FreeSVG](https://freesvg.org/) (CC0/public domain; free for commercial use). Exact artwork source, word, local filename and concept are documented individually in [`src/web/vocabularyDailyRoutineSources.json`](../../src/web/vocabularyDailyRoutineSources.json).

During publication, the images are downloaded, dimension-checked and converted into locally hosted 608×384 WebP scenes. Unmatched words use the existing Artico PNG illustrations, not an icon collage.
