# CH52 — In the Workplace

**Outcome:** Coordinate a routine or shift change, report a work problem, and confirm the agreed next action.  

## Study

Useful words include **shift**, a scheduled period of work; **break**, a pause in work; **staff meeting**, a meeting for employees; and **coverage**, having someone available to perform the needed work. **Clock in** and **clock out** refer to recording the start or end of work through a workplace's system.

### Coordinate time

Use this fictional team schedule: the staff meeting is Tuesday at 4 p.m.; Maya works Friday morning; Lee works Friday afternoon. Any shift change needs Jordan's confirmation for this practice team.

**Maya:** What time is the staff meeting?  
**Lee:** Four on Tuesday.  
**Maya:** Thanks. Is that every week?  
**Lee:** Yes, every Tuesday.  
**Maya:** I'll put it on my calendar.  

**Every week**, **once a month**, and **on Tuesdays** describe different timing patterns. Use Chapter 11's time support when needed.

### Request a change

**Maya:** I have a conflict on Friday morning. Could we discuss changing my shift?  
**Jordan:** What hours could you work instead?  
**Maya:** Friday afternoon. Lee and I have discussed a possible swap.  
**Jordan:** I need to check coverage before confirming it.  
**Maya:** Okay. When should we expect a decision?  
**Jordan:** I'll let you both know this afternoon.  

A **conflict** here means another obligation prevents you from attending. **Swap shifts** means exchange scheduled periods. Talking about a possible swap does not mean it has been approved. Confirm the result: **So I'm still scheduled for the morning until you confirm the change?**

If the change is refused, you can ask **Are there any other options?** or state **I understand. I still need help resolving the conflict**. A firmer request is **I need a clear answer by this afternoon so I can plan**. The reason and urgency can be stated without assuming a right to a particular arrangement.

### Report a problem

**Employee:** The checkout screen keeps freezing. I can't finish this order.  
**Supervisor:** When did it start?  
**Employee:** About ten minutes ago. It happened twice.  
**Supervisor:** I'll contact the support team.  
**Employee:** What should I do with the waiting order?  
**Supervisor:** I'll help you follow our backup process.  

**Keeps freezing** describes a repeated problem. **Won't turn on** describes a device failing to start. **Isn't working** is broad; add what you observe when possible. **I can't complete the task until...** explains the effect of a missing item or decision.

**Employee:** I don't have the final price list, so I can't send the quote yet.  
**Coworker:** I'll ask for it.  
**Employee:** Thanks. Could you let me know when it arrives?  

## Practice

Use the team schedule above. Alone, take both roles and speak the response before reading its model.

1. Ask when the staff meeting occurs and confirm whether it is weekly.
2. Request Maya's Friday shift change. Jordan says **I can't confirm it yet.** Ask when to expect a decision and confirm the current schedule.
3. The change is unavailable. Ask about another option without claiming it was already approved.
4. Your checkout screen froze twice in ten minutes. Describe the problem, its effect, and the help you need.

**Sample possibilities:** “Four on Tuesday, every week?” / “When should I expect a decision? Am I still on the morning shift until then?” / “Are there other options?” / “The screen froze twice in ten minutes. I can't finish the order. What should I do with it while support checks the problem?”  

Return later with another schedule or problem. Check that you distinguished a proposal from a confirmed change and included observable facts in the problem report.
