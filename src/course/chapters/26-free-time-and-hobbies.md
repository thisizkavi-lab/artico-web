# CH26 — Free Time and Hobbies

**Your goal:** Name leisure activities, understand a few less familiar choices, and choose words for your own interests.  

## Study: Build your activity vocabulary

**Free time** is time available outside your responsibilities. A **hobby** is an activity you pursue for interest or enjoyment. An activity can be someone's hobby and another person's job. Pick useful words for your life; you do not need to enjoy every item.

| Activity words | Meaning or useful example |
| --- | --- |
| Walking / running / hiking | Move on foot; hiking usually involves a longer outdoor walk, often on trails |
| Biking / cycling | Ride a bicycle; **mountain biking** uses a bike on rough, often off-road routes |
| Swimming / sailing | Move through water / travel in a boat driven by sails |
| Camping / having a picnic | Stay outdoors in a tent or other temporary setup / eat a meal outdoors |
| Skiing / snowboarding | Move over snow on skis / on a snowboard |
| Skateboarding / jumping rope | Ride a skateboard / jump over a rope you swing |
| Birdwatching / fishing | Observe birds / try to catch fish |
| Horseback riding / climbing | Ride a horse / move up a rock, wall or other surface |

Other options include **yoga**, **tai chi**, **flying a kite**, **going to an amusement park**, and **taking children to a playground**. For a more specialized interest, **orienteering** uses a map and compass to navigate a route; **foraging** involves looking for and gathering things such as wild foods. These names identify activities; they are not instructions for carrying them out.

## Study: Games and making things

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Board game | A game played using a board; chess is one example |
| Cards | Playing cards, or a game using them |
| Jigsaw puzzle | Separate pieces fitted together to make a picture |
| Gaming | Playing games, especially video games in this context |
| Darts | Throw small pointed darts at a target board |
| Dominoes | A game using marked rectangular tiles |
| Painting / drawing | Make pictures with paint / lines made with a pen, pencil or similar tool |
| Knitting / sewing | Make fabric from yarn using needles / join or repair material with stitches |
| Pottery | Make objects such as bowls from clay |
| Photography / gardening | Make photographs / grow and care for plants |

**Crafting** covers making things by hand. **Baking** can also be a hobby. To identify an object, **chessboard** names the board and **chess pieces** names the things moved on it.

## Study: Entertainment and music

A **movie theater** shows movies; a **theater** can also be a place for live performances. A **concert** is a music performance. A **festival** is an organized event or series of events, often with several performances. A **nightclub** is a venue for nighttime entertainment, often music and dancing.

A **band** is a group of musicians. An **orchestra** is a larger organized instrumental group; a **choir** is a group of singers. **Ballet** is a form of dance, while **opera** combines staged drama with music and singing.

Music labels include **pop**, **rock**, **country**, **hip-hop**, **dance music**, **jazz**, and **classical music**. **Bhangra** is another name you may encounter, associated with Punjabi dance and music. Labels can overlap; you can name an artist or song if a category does not describe your taste well.

For a brief activity sentence, use **I play chess**, **I go hiking**, **I do yoga**, or **I like gardening**. These are examples of useful combinations, not a rule that every activity takes the same verb. Chapter 33 develops a longer conversation about hobbies.

## Practice: Select and retrieve

Speak aloud or write a short answer. All the descriptions you need are above.

1. Name an activity from each clue: riding a bicycle; fitting pieces into a picture; making bowls from clay; watching birds. Check the matching table rows afterward.
2. Choose three activities from different groups. Say one short sentence for each using a supplied combination or **I like…**.
3. For a fictional role, choose between a hiking trail, a pottery workshop and a chess club. Explain the chosen activity in simple words. Support: **I'd choose…**, **You…**, **It involves…**.
4. Change your role's preference: the person who wanted an outdoor activity now wants an indoor one. Choose a different activity and name what changed.
5. Close the tables. Name two making activities, two outdoor activities and one performance. Return to the table for a word you forgot, then explain that word without copying its definition.

### Sample possibilities

1. **Biking or cycling; a jigsaw puzzle; pottery; birdwatching.**
2. **I play chess. I go hiking. I like photography.**
3. **I'd choose the pottery workshop. You make things from clay.**
4. **I'd choose chess instead. I want an indoor activity today.**
5. **Knitting and painting; swimming and camping; a concert.** Other choices from the tables also fit.

**Check:** Did the name match the activity? Could you use a few words in a meaningful sentence? Did your changed choice fit the changed preference?
