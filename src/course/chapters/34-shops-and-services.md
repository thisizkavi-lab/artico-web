# CH34 — Shops and Services

**Outcome:** Recognize useful places and payment words, then use a selected set to explain what you need.  

## Study

These words support the service conversations in Chapters 35–44. Begin with places you are likely to use; the list is a reference, not a memorization test.

| Place | What the word identifies |
| --- | --- |
| grocery store / supermarket | a store selling food and everyday supplies |
| convenience store | a smaller store selling a range of everyday items |
| hardware store | a store selling tools and home-project supplies |
| garden center | a place selling plants and gardening supplies |
| gas station | a place where vehicles can get fuel |
| shopping mall | a group of stores in a shopping complex |
| pharmacy | a place to ask about or obtain medicines |
| post office | a place providing postal services |
| library | a place offering books and other information resources |
| bank | a business providing financial services |

Other useful store names are **bakery** for bread and baked goods, **butcher shop** for meat, **florist** for flowers, **bookstore** for books, **shoe store** for footwear, and **hair salon** or **barbershop** for hair services. A store may sell more than its name suggests; ask when you are unsure.

### People and places inside

A **customer** buys or uses a service. A **cashier** handles checkout. A **sales associate** helps customers in a store. **Checkout** is the place or process of paying. An **aisle** is a passage between shelves. A **line** is people waiting their turn: **Is this the line for checkout?**

### Money and purchases

| Word | Meaning in a shopping task |
| --- | --- |
| cash | physical money: bills and coins |
| debit card | a card that uses money in a linked bank or credit-union account |
| credit card | a card that lets you borrow money for purchases and repay it later |
| receipt | a record of a purchase or payment |
| sale | a period or offer with reduced prices |
| refund | money returned after a purchase |
| exchange | replacing an item with another item |
| ATM | a machine for certain banking transactions |
| contactless payment | payment made by bringing a compatible card or device near a reader |

Useful combinations are **pay for a jacket**, **get a receipt**, **return an item**, and **ask for a refund**. Asking does not establish that a particular store will grant the request.

**A:** I'm looking for a bookstore.  
**B:** There's one near the library.  
**A:** Thanks. And is there an ATM nearby?  
**B:** I don't know, but you could ask at the bank.  

## Practice

1. Choose the place: you want a loaf of bread; a hammer; a haircut; information about mailing a box. Say **I'm looking for...** with each answer.
2. Choose the word: a purchase record; money returned; people waiting; the passage between shelves.
3. Use this fictional directory: Pine Bakery, Lake Hardware, Oak Pharmacy, West Post Office. Ask where to go for nails, bread, or mailing help. Take both roles if practicing alone.

**Sample possibilities:** “I'm looking for a bakery / hardware store / hair salon / post office.” The vocabulary answers are **receipt, refund, line, aisle**. “Where can I buy nails?” — “Try Lake Hardware.”  

Later, cover the lists and describe a task with three useful words. Check whether your listener could identify the place or need; remembering every entry is unnecessary.
