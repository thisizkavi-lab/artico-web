# CH21 — Cafés and Coffee Shops

**Your goal:** Order a drink or snack, answer the options offered, and manage a simple pickup or seating question.  

## Study: Build an order

**Could I have...?**, **Can I get...?**, and **I'll have...** can begin an order. Specify the item and any detail that matters: **a small latte with oat milk**, **a black coffee**, or **an iced tea with no sugar**.

This invented menu supplies the choices for the lesson. Prices are practice data, not prices for a real café.

| Item | Small | Large |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee | $3.00 | $3.75 |
| Latte | $4.75 | $5.50 |
| Iced tea | $3.50 | $4.25 |

**Options:** dairy milk or oat milk; oat milk adds $0.50. Coffee and lattes can be hot or iced. **Snack:** a vegetable sandwich, $6.00. In this fictional scene, staff describe its ingredients as bread, vegetables and hummus with no animal-derived ingredients.  

**At the counter**

**Staff:** Hi. What can I get you?  
**Customer:** A small iced latte with oat milk, please.  
**Staff:** For here or to go?  
**Customer:** To go.  
**Staff:** Can I get a name for the order?  
**Customer:** Robin.  
**Staff:** Small iced latte with oat milk for Robin.  
**Customer:** That's right.  

**For here** means you will have it at the café. **To go** means you want to take it away. The name helps identify the order in this scene; not every shop uses the same pickup system.

## Study: Answer options and check details

- **Small or large? — Small, please.**
- **Hot or iced? — Iced.**
- **Any milk or sugar? — A little milk, no sugar.**
- **Would you like the sandwich toasted? — Yes, please.**
- **Anything else? — No, that's all, thanks.**

If you want a particular milk, name it: **Do you have skim milk?** or **Could you make it with oat milk?** This is clearer than assuming every shop uses the same meaning for a menu label such as **skinny**.

**Checking a sandwich**

**Customer:** Does the vegetable sandwich contain any animal ingredients?  
**Staff:** Not according to this recipe. It has bread, vegetables and hummus.  
**Customer:** Okay. I'd like one, toasted, please.  

For an allergy or a question about preparation, use the more specific language in Chapter 24. A preference question and an allergy question are not necessarily the same task.

## Study: Handle an unavailable option

**Staff:** Sorry, we're out of oat milk. We have dairy milk.  
**Customer:** I'll have a small black coffee instead.  
**Staff:** Hot or iced?  
**Customer:** Hot, please.  

**We're out of...** means the item is unavailable now. You can choose an alternative, ask about another option, or say **I'll pass, thanks**. Do not continue confirming an order that is no longer available.

## Study: Pickup and staying at the café

**Is that the latte for Robin?** checks a drink before taking it. **I ordered a hot coffee, but this is iced** identifies a mistake.

With a companion, **I'll wait for the drinks. Can you find a table?** divides the task. **Is this seat taken? — No, go ahead** asks about a seat. **Are refills included?** checks the shop's policy rather than assumes a refill is free. **Do you have a rewards card? — No, I don't** is a possible staff question and reply.

## Practice: Complete the order

Use the menu and options above. On your own, speak the staff questions and your answers, then switch roles.

1. Order a small hot latte with oat milk for here. Answer questions about size, milk, location and name. Use an invented name if you prefer.
2. Add the vegetable sandwich, toasted. End the order when staff ask **Anything else?**
3. Change the staff response: oat milk is unavailable. Choose another listed drink or decline the order.
4. Your drink is called, but you want to check whose it is. Ask. Staff answer **Yes, that's Robin's order**.
5. The coffee you ordered hot is served iced. Explain the mismatch and ask **Could you replace it with a hot one?** Staff reply **Yes. Sorry about that**.
6. Hide the first model. Make a different complete order using the menu, and rehearse one seating or refill question afterward.

### Sample possibilities

1. **Can I get a small hot latte with oat milk, please? — For here or to go? — For here. — Name? — Robin.**
2. **And a toasted vegetable sandwich, please. — Anything else? — No, that's all, thanks.**
3. **I'll have an iced tea instead** or **I'll pass, thanks**.
4. **Is that the latte for Robin?**
5. **I ordered a hot coffee, but this is iced. Could you replace it with a hot one?**

**Check:** Did your final order include the options you wanted? Could you respond when an option disappeared? Did your pickup or correction question identify the item clearly?
