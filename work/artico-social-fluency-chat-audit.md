## Editorial implication

The legacy chat is useful source material, but it is not the book. The 18 existing responses must be moved into the manuscript chapter by chapter and rewritten into a consistent Artico structure: Can-do, context, full Japanese theory, register and nuance, dialogue, Japanese-learner friction, culture, Good to Know, practice bridge, audio brief, and image brief.

Missing chapters must be written from scratch. They must not be silently represented as completed merely because the source book contains the topic.
