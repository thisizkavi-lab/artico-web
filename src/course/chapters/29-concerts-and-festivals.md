# CH29 — Concerts and Festivals

**Your goal:** Find performers and facilities, understand an entry request, and arrange a meeting at a busy event.  

## Study: Understand the schedule

A **lineup** names the performers at an event. The **headliner** is the main featured act. An **opening act** or **opener** performs before the main act. **Gig** is an informal word for a musician's performance or engagement. A **set** is the group of songs performed during a stretch of the show.

Use this fictional Pine Creek Festival information throughout Practice:

| Detail | Festival information |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday entry | Gates open at 2:30 p.m. |
| Park stage | Free classical concert at 3 p.m.; no reservation needed |
| Main stage | Juniper, the opening band, at 6 p.m.; Northbound, the headliner, at 8 p.m. |
| Entry procedure | Staff inspect bags and check festival wristbands |
| Bottle rule | Glass bottles are not allowed; an empty metal water bottle is allowed |
| Facilities | Food trucks beside the main stage; restrooms beside the north gate |
| Camping | Camping area opens Friday at noon; assigned sites have a row letter and number |

These are this invented festival's conditions. Another event may have different ticketing, entry or camping arrangements.

**Guest:** Who's playing on the main stage tonight?  
**Staff:** Juniper at six, then Northbound at eight.  
**Guest:** Is Juniper the opener?  
**Staff:** Yes.  

**What time does the headliner start?** asks about the main act, not when the gates open. **Can I see the full lineup?** asks for the wider schedule. For the classical concert, **Do we need to reserve tickets?** checks whether you can simply arrive.

## Study: At the entrance

**Staff:** Could you have your bag ready for inspection?  
**Guest:** Sure. Is this empty metal bottle okay?  
**Staff:** Yes. We don't allow glass bottles.  
**Guest:** Got it.  
**Staff:** Can I see your wristband, please?  
**Guest:** Here it is.  

An **inspection** is a check; a **wristband** is a band worn around your wrist, here showing permission to enter. **Could you explain what isn't allowed?** asks for clearer information. A question such as **Can I bring this in?** identifies the actual item rather than assume that every bottle or bag has the same rule.

## Study: Find food, restrooms or a campsite

**Where are the food trucks?** asks for the food sellers. **Where's the restroom?** asks about toilets. **The line is really long** describes a wait; **Let's try another one** proposes a different option if one is available.

**Camper:** I can't find my tent. My site is B12.  
**Staff:** Which row are you in now?  
**Camper:** Row A.  
**Staff:** Look for the row B sign. Then follow that row to number twelve.  
**Camper:** Thanks. Row B, number twelve.  

A **campsite** or **site** here means the assigned place for your tent. Give a useful identifier rather than ask staff to recognize **my tent** without any details. **Is the camping area open yet?** asks about access to that area.

## Study: Separate and meet again

At a crowded event, **I'll meet you later** leaves time and location open. Supply both when you need a definite plan.

**Alex:** I'm going to the food trucks. Want to come?  
**Riley:** I'll stay for the rest of this set. Let's meet by the north gate at seven thirty.  
**Alex:** Okay, by the north gate at seven thirty. Then we'll go to the main stage for Northbound.  
**Riley:** Sounds good.  

If you cannot hear, **It's loud here. Could you text me the meeting place?** requests a different way to receive the information. If delayed, **I'm still in the food line. Can we meet at seven forty-five instead?** proposes a changed time that the other person can accept or reject.

## Study: React to a performance

**What an amazing performance!** and **That set was great** express enjoyment. **I loved the band, but I couldn't hear the vocals clearly** gives a mixed view. **It was way too loud for me** is a strong personal reaction. There is no requirement to give the same reaction as a companion.

**Encore!** calls for another performance after the main performance finishes. An **encore** is that additional performance. A call does not guarantee that the musicians will return.

## Practice: Navigate an event

Speak both roles alone. Use the festival table and the row directions above.

1. Ask who opens for Northbound and when each band starts. Staff give the schedule. Choose which performance you want to attend.
2. At entry, staff request your bag and wristband. Ask whether your empty metal bottle is allowed; staff answer from the rule.
3. Change the item: you have a glass bottle. Ask about it; staff say it cannot come in. Respond **Okay, I won't bring it in** rather than treat the other bottle's permission as applying to it.
4. You are in row A but your tent site is B12. Ask for help, give the site number, and repeat the direction to check it.
5. One person wants food; the other stays for the set. Arrange the north-gate meeting at 7:30. Change the response: the food line delays one person. Propose 7:45 and have the other accept or reject it. Support: **That works**, **That's too late for me; let's keep seven thirty**.
6. Hide the models. Ask about the free classical concert's reservation requirement, then reconstruct an entry or meeting exchange. Finish with a positive, negative or mixed reaction to an imagined performance.

### Sample possibilities

1. **Who's opening for Northbound? — Juniper, at six. Northbound starts at eight.**
2. **Here you go. Can I bring this empty metal bottle in? — Yes.**
3. **Okay, I won't bring the glass bottle in.**
4. **My site is B12, but I'm in row A. — Look for row B, then number twelve. — Row B, number twelve. Got it.**
5. **Can we meet by the north gate at seven thirty?** Later: **I'm delayed. Would seven forty-five work? — Yes, that works.**
6. **Do we need to reserve tickets? — No, you can just come.** Reaction: **The band was great, but I couldn't hear the words clearly.**

**Check:** Did you distinguish gate time from performance time? Did you identify the actual item or campsite? Did the revised meeting have both a place and an agreed time?
