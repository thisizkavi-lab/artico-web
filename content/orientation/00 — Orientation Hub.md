# Orientation

The orientation is the threshold of Artico. It should give a Japanese learner a clear reason to learn English, a realistic picture of how learning works, and a simple way to use the course.

This is not another grammar chapter. It is the philosophy and the operating manual for the learner.

## Editorial decisions already made

- Orientation length is determined by coherence and usefulness, not an arbitrary minute count.
- The launch guidance language is Japanese, but the architecture supports learners from anywhere.
- Theory can be shown in Japanese, English, or bilingual form.
- Practice stays English-forward, with optional support-language instructions and hints.
- Content is populated and reviewed before the UI is finalized.

## How we will write it

You ramble first. I preserve the important ideas, remove repetition, check logic, and shape the material into a readable chapter. Your voice remains the source; the outline is only the container.
