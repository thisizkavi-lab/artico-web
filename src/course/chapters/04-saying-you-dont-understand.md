# CH04 — When Something Isn't Clear

**Your goal:** Ask someone to repeat, explain a word, or say something another way.

## Study: You didn't catch something

Use these when you didn't hear a word or detail clearly.

| Phrase | Example in use | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Sorry?** | **Sorry?** | Ask someone to repeat a short part. |
| **Sorry, I didn't catch that.** | **Sorry, I didn't catch your name.** | Say you didn't hear something clearly. |
| **Could you say that again?** | **Could you say that again**, please? | Ask someone to repeat what they said. |
| **Did you say ...?** | **Did you say eight?** | Check a word, number, or detail. |

**Maya:** The class starts at eight.  
**Sam:** Sorry, **did you say eight?**  
**Maya:** Yes, eight.  
**Sam:** Okay, thanks.

## Study: You heard it, but don't understand

Use these when you hear the words but don't know what they mean.

| Phrase | Example in use | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **What does ... mean?** | **What does "hang out" mean?** | Ask about a word or phrase you don't know. |
| **I don't understand.** | Sorry, **I don't understand this word.** | Say that something isn't clear to you. |
| **Oh, I see.** | **Oh, I see.** Thanks! | Show that you understand now. |

**Maya:** Let's hang out after class.  
**Sam:** **What does "hang out" mean?**  
**Maya:** Spend time together.  
**Sam:** **Oh, I see!**

## Study: You need a different explanation

Use these when someone speaks too fast or the explanation isn't clear.

| Phrase | Example in use | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Could you slow down a little?** | **Could you slow down a little**, please? | Ask someone to speak more slowly. |
| **Could you explain that again?** | **Could you explain that again**, please? | Ask someone to repeat an explanation. |
| **Could you say that another way?** | **Could you say that another way**, please? | Ask for a different, simpler explanation. |
| **Could you give me an example?** | **Could you give me an example**, please? | Ask for a simple example. |

**Maya:** Take the second left.  
**Sam:** Sorry, **could you say that another way?**  
**Maya:** Turn left at the second street.  
**Sam:** Oh, okay. Thanks.

## Practice: Try it

1. You didn't hear a time. Ask the person to repeat it.
2. You hear a new phrase. Ask what it means.
3. The directions aren't clear. Ask the person to explain another way.
