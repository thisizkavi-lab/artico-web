# CH66 — Buses and Intercity Buses

**Outcome:** Check a bus's destination, ask about payment, identify your stop, and ask about a longer trip.  

## Study

A **route** is the path or service a bus follows. The **fare** is the price of travel. **Downtown** names a city's central area. **Next stop** is the first stop coming up; **last bus** means the final departure in a stated period.

### Check before getting on

**Passenger:** Excuse me, does this bus go to the art museum?  
**Driver:** Not this one. You need the number 8, across the street.  
**Passenger:** The number 8 at that stop?  
**Driver:** Yes, by the pharmacy.  
**Passenger:** Thanks. What time does it come?  
**Driver:** The next one is at 9:10.  

“Is this the right stop for the airport bus?” checks your location. “Does this bus stop at the mall?” checks a stop on the route. “Which bus goes downtown?” asks for a service. Confirm the detail: “Across the street?” A short confirmation can prevent taking the wrong bus.

### Ask about the fare

**Passenger:** How much is the fare to the museum?  
**Driver:** Two fifty for this trip.  
**Passenger:** Can I pay with my phone?  
**Driver:** Yes. Use the reader here.  
**Passenger:** Do I need to tap again when I get off?  
**Driver:** No, not on this route.  

These are fictional conditions. Payment methods, transfers, and tapping rules vary. Useful alternatives are “Do you take cash?” and “Is a transfer included?” A **transfer** is changing to another service; an included transfer concerns ticket conditions, not just the physical change.

### Find your stop

**Passenger:** Could you let me know when we get to the museum?  
**Driver:** Sure. It's after Central Library.  
**Passenger:** Are we there yet?  
**Driver:** Not yet. Two more stops.  

Use “I think I missed my stop. Where should I get off?” if your plan has gone wrong. With a friend, “Is this our stop?” is a compact check. It does not mean you are certain.

### Longer trips

**Agent:** May I see your ticket?  
**Traveler:** Here it is. Is there a restroom on the bus?  
**Agent:** Yes, at the back.  
**Traveler:** Is Wi-Fi available?  
**Agent:** Not on this departure.  
**Traveler:** Okay. Where does my suitcase go?  
**Agent:** I'll show you the luggage compartment.  

**Restroom**, **luggage compartment**, and **Wi-Fi** describe facilities. Ask rather than assuming they are provided.

## Practice

Fictional stop card: Route 8 goes to the museum at 9:10 and 9:40; its last departure is 8:30 p.m. Route 12 goes to the airport at 9:20. Route 8's fare is $2.50, paid by cash or phone; no transfer is included.

1. You want the museum. Ask the driver whether Route 12 is right. Partner reply: “This is the airport bus.” Correct your plan.
2. Ask about the fare and a transfer for Route 8. Partner uses the card. Confirm one detail.
3. On a longer trip, ask about a restroom and luggage. Partner reply: “The restroom is at the back. Your suitcase goes underneath.” Acknowledge or clarify.

Alone, read the partner lines, cover the models, and supply your own turns. Later change the destination.

**Possibilities:** “Which bus goes to the museum?” “So it's two fifty, and a transfer isn't included?” “Do you mean the luggage compartment under the bus?” Check that you establish the route and payment conditions; the wording can vary.
