# CH14 — Family and Relationships

**Your goal:** Choose family and relationship words that accurately describe a person or connection.  

## Study: Close and extended family

These words describe relationships, not how close people feel to each other. Begin with the ones you need for your own account or an invented family.

| Words | Short meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **parent; mother / mom; father / dad** | Your mother or father is a parent. Adoptive parents are parents too. Mom and Dad are personal family names. |
| **child; son; daughter** | A child in relation to a parent; son and daughter identify family roles, including adult children. |
| **sibling; brother; sister** | Sibling is a general word for a brother or sister. |
| **grandparent; grandmother; grandfather** | A parent's parent. |
| **grandchild; granddaughter; grandson** | A child's child. |
| **aunt; uncle** | A parent's sibling, or the spouse of a parent's sibling. |
| **first cousin** | A child of your parent's sibling. Cousin also has broader family uses. |
| **niece; nephew** | Your sibling's daughter or son; also used for a spouse's sibling's children. |
| **spouse; husband; wife** | Spouse is a general word for a married partner. |
| **mother-in-law; father-in-law** | Your spouse's mother or father. |
| **brother-in-law; sister-in-law** | Your spouse's sibling, or your sibling's spouse. |

**Only child** means a person with no siblings. **Oldest**, **middle**, and **youngest** describe order among children. **Twins** are two children from the same pregnancy; the word does not mean they must look identical.

## Study: Step- and half- relationships

An **ex-husband** or **ex-wife** is a former spouse. A **stepparent** is a parent's spouse who is not your other birth parent; the words **stepmother**, **stepfather**, **stepmom**, and **stepdad** identify specific roles or family names.

A **stepbrother** or **stepsister** is a stepparent's child from another relationship. A **half-brother** or **half-sister** shares one birth parent with you. The labels differ; neither tells you how people feel about one another.

**An invented family:** Sam was born to Rae and Kim. Rae later married Lee. Lee already had a daughter, Ava, from another relationship. Rae and Lee then had a son, Noah.  

- Ava is Sam's **stepsister**.
- Noah is Sam's **half-brother**: they share Rae as a birth parent.

People may use a simpler label such as **my brother** when that expresses their family connection. You can explain a detail when it matters rather than list every distinction immediately.

## Study: Other relationships and life stages

A **partner** can be a person in a romantic relationship; the word also has work and activity meanings. **Engaged** means planning to marry. **Married**, **divorced**, and **single** describe relationship status; in dating conversation, single may mean not currently in a relationship, rather than only a legal marital status. A **single parent** raises a child without a co-parenting partner in the household; actual family arrangements vary.

A **friend** is a personal connection; a **colleague** is someone you work with; a **neighbor** lives nearby. The same person can have more than one connection to you.

Life-stage words include **baby**, **toddler** (a young child beginning to walk), **child**, **teenager** (a person aged thirteen to nineteen), **adult**, and **older adult**. **Senior citizen** is another term you may encounter for an older person; this chapter sets no exact age threshold.

Useful event words are **birth**, **birthday**, **graduation** (completing a course of study), **promotion** (moving to a higher work position), **wedding**, **divorce**, **retirement** (leaving working life), and **funeral** (a ceremony associated with someone's death). **Vacation** can describe time off or a leisure trip. Chapter 16 practices responding to events.

## Practice: Retrieve a useful subset

1. Use the invented family above. Complete: **Ava is Sam's ___. Noah is Sam's ___.** Explain the difference in a sentence.
2. Your sister has a son, Leo. What is Leo's relationship to you? Your child has a daughter, Mia. What is Mia's relationship to you?
3. A person says **My daughter is thirty**. Explain why daughter does not mean only a young child.
4. Pick four words from different parts of Study. Describe an invented person or family using them. Support: **I have...**, **This is my...**, **She's my...**, **We work together**.
5. Hide the definitions. Describe one connection without saying its label. Then identify the word from your description. On your own, make the description, pause, and answer it yourself.

### Sample possibilities

1. **Stepsister; half-brother. Ava is Lee's daughter from an earlier relationship. Noah and Sam share Rae as a birth parent.**
2. **Leo is your nephew. Mia is your granddaughter.**
3. **Daughter describes her relationship to a parent, even when she is an adult.**
4. **I'm the oldest. I have one sister. My colleague is also my friend.**

**Check:** Did the word describe the actual connection? Could you distinguish step- and half- relationships when the distinction mattered? Choose a manageable subset rather than test yourself on every term at once.
