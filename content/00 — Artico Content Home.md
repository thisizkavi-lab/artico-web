## Current editorial truth

- The spine is **96 chapters**, grouped into **11 parts**.
- Only **18 chapters** currently have substantial legacy theory in the NotebookLM chat.
- **78 chapters** still need original chapter writing.
- “Theory” means an explanation of why, when, and how the language works—not a list of phrases.
- Video is intentionally not part of this pass.
- Orientation length is not fixed; coherence comes before a runtime target.
- Theory guidance can be Japanese, English, or bilingual; practice is English-forward with optional support.
- The launch is Japan-centric, but the content architecture is designed for learners from anywhere.

## Workflow

1. Open a chapter note.
2. Write and review the Japanese theory.
3. Add the original dialogue and practice bridge.
4. Create a natural theory audio brief.
5. Create a shadowing practice audio brief.
6. Generate audio in Google AI Studio, then clean and level it in Audacity if needed.
7. Add an image only when it improves understanding.
8. Derive the app lesson only after the chapter is book-ready.

## Naming

Chapter notes use `NN — English title.md`. Audio uses:
